Tino Science Olympiad

23 events. One team effort.

A rigorous, team-based STEM competition. Students prepare for written tests, lab work and engineering builds, and the whole team's score counts.

Tryouts & how to join Competition schedule
What members do

Pick about three events and go deep

There are 23 events across five categories. Each student works on about three of them. Depending on the event, that means a written test, a build, lab work or designing a device. Teams compete at invitationals and regionals, and can advance to state and nationals.

How teams prepare

Preparation is on you and your partner

Partners prepare mostly on their own: studying event material, taking practice tests, running experiments, and building and testing devices. Some events require a tested device, cheat sheet or binder at the tournament.

Officers guide teams and track deadlines, but each partnership owns its own preparation and contributes equally.

3 events per student, each prepared with a partner
Why join

Science far beyond the curriculum

  • Explore fields the classroom never reaches, a strong start for future STEM majors
  • Hands-on work: binders, vehicles and real lab procedures
  • A community of passionate, like-minded teammates
  • Medals, awards and real competition experience
  • Leadership skills and the chance to apply for officer positions
