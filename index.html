    About the club

    The science club at CHS

    Tino Science is a student-run club for anyone at Tino who wants to go further in science. The club runs two competition teams, Science Olympiad and Science Bowl. Officers organize tryouts, coach the teams and keep everyone on track for tournaments.

    Our teams

    Two ways to compete

    Science Olympiad

    Tino Science Olympiad is a rigorous team-based STEM competition in which high school students prepare for a total of 23 events across different subjects such as biology, chemistry, physics, astronomy, Earth science, and engineering. Students work on approximately 3 specialized events that can either be written, build, laboratory based or related to designing devices. Teams get the opportunity to compete at invitationals and regional tournaments, with further opportunities to advance to state and national competitions.

    Science Bowl

    Science Bowl is an exciting, fast-paced buzzer competition with 5 members/team! The game operates like jeopardy with toss-up and bonus questions, except with a focus on science. Questions cycle between Math, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Earth and Space Science. Each member typically specializes deeply in 1 or 2 subjects. Cupertino’s SciBowl team usually participates in over 6 tournaments a year, including both in-person and online tournaments with live scoreboards and rankings.

    Tryouts & joining

    Two teams. Two tryouts.

    Both teams hold tryouts at the start of the school year. Exact dates change each year, so watch the Instagram accounts and Tino News Today emails.

    Science Olympiad

    Joining SciOly

    1. Take the tryout test. It is usually held in September and checks fundamental knowledge.
    2. Holistic review. The test matters, but officers also consider past achievements, experience and progress during summer sessions.
    3. Team selection. Two teams of 15 are formed. Other applicants are not placed on a team this season.
    4. Start preparing. Build foundations for your events, begin your cheat sheet, binder or device, and stay in touch with your partner.
    Science Bowl

    Joining SciBowl

    1. Pick your subjects. Tryouts are by subject. Try out for just one, for example only chemistry, or for all five.
    2. Watch for the announcement. Tryouts are at the beginning of the year and the format can change. Check @tinosciencebowl and Tino News Today.
    3. Join the mailing list. Sign up at tinyurl.com/tsb2627 to hear about tryouts and practices for 2026–27.

    New to Science Olympiad? This year SciOly ran summer bootcamp sessions for incoming freshmen and students new to the competition. Attendees got a head start on events, and their attendance and progress counted toward team selection.

    FAQ

    Common questions

    Do I need experience to try out?

    No. SciOly runs summer bootcamp sessions for students new to Science Olympiad, and officers weigh progress alongside test results. SciBowl tryouts let you focus on just the subjects you know best.

    Can I try out for both teams?

    Tryouts are held separately for each team. If you are interested in both, email the club and the officers will point you to the right sessions.

    How much time does it take?

    SciBowl practices twice a week plus team bonding. SciOly partners set their own study routine, and teams must attend every invitational and the March regional.

    Who do I ask if I have questions?

    Email the club at tinoscienceclub@gmail.com, or reach Science Bowl directly at tinosciencebowl@gmail.com.
