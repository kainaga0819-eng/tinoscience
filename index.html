About Us

Tino Science is a club dedicated to providing opportunities for members to further their interests in science and STEM; various fun/hands-on activities and guest speaker events will take place throughout the year. The club also prepares members for the national STEM Olympiads, including USABO, USNCO, USAPhO, and Science Olympiad, and members also compete in Science Bowl and the Synopsys Science Fair.
